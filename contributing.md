## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is this a free VPN?</b></summary>

Lightspeed Bypass Proxy is an **open-source client**. To use the global server network, you will need a subscription to a compatible proxy provider. The client itself is free to download and use with your own self-hosted servers.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use this for torrenting?</b></summary>

Yes, but make sure the server you are connected to supports P2P traffic. We recommend enabling the Kill Switch to prevent your real IP from leaking if the connection drops.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will it slow down my internet?</b></summary>

Any encrypted tunnel adds a tiny amount of overhead. However, Lightspeed uses the highly efficient QUIC protocol, which means you'll typically experience less than a 5% drop in speed compared to your raw connection.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work in China or Russia?</b></summary>

While no proxy is 100% bulletproof against sophisticated state firewalls, Lightspeed includes an "Obfuscation Mode" specifically designed to bypass Deep Packet Inspection (DPI). It has a high success rate in restricted regions.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug?</b></summary>

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include your OS, network environment, and the error logs from the app.
</details>
